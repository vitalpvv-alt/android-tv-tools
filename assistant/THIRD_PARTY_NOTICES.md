## Home weather data

Weather forecasts by Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/ , and MET Norway,
https://www.met.no/ , under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
Weather text is translated to Russian and numeric values are rounded for display.
Daily MET Norway ranges are calculated from forecast points for the selected local day.
Open-Meteo's free service is used for this personal home automation installation.
Sources: https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast and
https://api.met.no/weatherapi/locationforecast/2.0/compact . No AI credentials are used.
